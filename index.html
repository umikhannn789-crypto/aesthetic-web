The Power of Carbon Peel

Deep pore cleansing, blackhead extraction, sebum reduction, collagen stimulation and evening out hyperpigmentation for an instant red-carpet glass glow.

  • Exfoliates & refreshes dull skin
  • Shrinks enlarged facial pores
  • Zero downtime, immediate glow

Hair Restoration & FUE

Permanent solutions for baldness. Micro-graft follicular extraction restoring natural hairlines with dense, permanent root growth.

  • Natural angle follicular grafting
  • Painless local anaesthesia technique
  • Free scalp analysis included

Laser Hair Removal

Confidence in every session with ice-chill contact cooling. Smooth, hair-free skin without irritation, ingrown hairs or waxing burns.

  • Safe on all skin tones
  • Full body packages available
  • FDA-certified laser technology

PRP Therapy & Microneedling

Harness your body's concentrated growth factors to revitalise inactive hair follicles, minimise stubborn acne pitting and restore youthfulness.

  • Autologous pure plasma extraction
  • Dermapen micro-channel collagen boost
  • Great for hair thinning and scars

Medical HydraFacials

Multi-step vortex suction and serum infusion with hyaluronic acid, peptides and botanical antioxidants for deep moisture and luminescence.

  • Deep extraction without red marks
  • Intense hydration & glow infusion
  • Perfect before weddings & events
